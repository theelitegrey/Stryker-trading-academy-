# Brag Plan: Stryker Trading Academy

## What is this app?
Stryker Trading Academy is a 42-chapter ICT / Smart Money trading curriculum wrapped in a
full trading desk: a trade journal with analytics, a prop-firm ledger, live market monitor,
in-house TradingView indicators and a "trading floor" community. The teacher's own funded
payouts are posted on the landing page as proof.

## The angle
The site's own thesis is the video's spine: **price leaves footprints, and the crowd
reads them too late.** Open on a chart doing exactly what the curriculum describes (a
liquidity sweep above equal highs, then a reversal), then let the product speak in its
own words: the hero headline, the chapter path, the journal doing its job, and the
payouts that back it up. No invented claims, no SaaS filler. Every line on screen is
copy that already exists on strykertrading.com.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A near-black frame. A candlestick chart draws itself left to right in the site's mint
green. Price runs up into a dotted "equal highs" line, wicks through it (the sweep),
and snaps back down. As the reversal candle lands, the line **"Price leaves footprints."**
slams in. That one sentence is the site's whole pitch and it earns the next 20 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero headline, verbatim: "Trade the way institutions actually **move price**." with
  the emphasized words in mint, and the three hero stats beneath it (42 chapters, 4.9/5 rating).
- Three chapter cards from the real curriculum preview arriving one by one:
  01 Candles, Charts & the Language of Price (Free) → 08 Market Structure & Break of
  Structure → 27 SMT Divergence Across Correlated Pairs (Advanced).
- The trade journal at work: a cursor hits **Save trade**, the equity curve extends, and
  the Win rate / Profit factor / Net P&L tiles tick up. The app doing its thing, not describing it.
- The proof strip: "Verified funded-account payouts." over the site's own payout
  certificate marquee.

## Outro / punchline
Dragon emblem lands on a strong beat. "Stryker Trading Academy". Then the site's own
footer line, quiet and small: **"Made for traders who read the tape."** and the URL.

## User flow worth showing
Log a trade → see the month at a glance. In the journal: the New trade form is filled
(EURUSD, Long, entry / exit), the cursor clicks **Save trade**, and the dashboard reacts:
the Cumulative P&L curve draws one more leg up, Net P&L counts up, Win rate and Profit
factor tick to new values. This is Scene 4, the centerpiece.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with a cinematic lean on the hook and outro)
- Creative direction: quiet, premium trading-desk film. Confidence through restraint.
- Interpretation: Fewer, longer holds. Mixed case, tight tracking, heavy display weight
  against monospace data. Motion is decisive but never busy. No jokes; the product is
  earnest and the video treats it that way. Transitions are soft blur crossfades with one
  hard beat-locked cut into the proof scene.
