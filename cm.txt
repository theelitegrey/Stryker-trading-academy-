Add security headers, and cut two third-party scripts off the site

Headers (_headers, applied by Cloudflare Pages to every response):

- Strict-Transport-Security, X-Frame-Options: DENY, Permissions-Policy and
  Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy. The site had none of these; without frame
  protection a clickjacking page can overlay an invisible dashboard and
  harvest clicks on real controls.
- A Content-Security-Policy shipped in REPORT-ONLY mode. It changes nothing
  for visitors and only logs violations, which is the right way to introduce
  one: load the pages signed in, read the console, widen the lists where the
  app legitimately needs an origin, then rename the header to start enforcing.
  The comment in the file says exactly that, and notes that 'unsafe-inline'
  in script-src is what currently limits its value.

Third-party scripts:

- lottie-web was loaded from a CDN on 61 pages and used by nothing. The
  loading animation has been pure CSS/SVG since it was rebuilt; the tag just
  never got removed. Deleted, along with the orphaned loading.json.
- chart.js is now served from assets/vendor/ instead of the CDN, so the two
  pages that draw journal charts no longer execute a script from an origin we
  do not control.

That is one fewer third-party origin on almost every page of the site, and
one of the two remaining CDN dependencies removed outright.

Verified: 24-page headless sweep clean, and the Chart global still resolves
on both trade-journal and messages.
