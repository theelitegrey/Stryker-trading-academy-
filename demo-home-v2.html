Questions

Before you start

Is TradeZoro free?
Yes. Every member gets ZoroCharts, Kronos, paper trading, Market Stats, Liquidity Map, Nemesis, all 64 Mentis chapters, Smart Money Desk, trading models and the community, free. Every tool is free too, order flow and Omega included. The only paid part is the live futures order book data feed: exchange rules set it, so we can't give it free.
Does TradeZoro place orders for me?
No. TradeZoro is for charts, data, learning and review. We never place orders. You trade at your own broker or prop firm.
What does the Rithmic connection do?
It brings the live futures data from your prop firm's Rithmic login onto ZoroCharts. It is data only. If you would rather not connect, live futures (L1) is free once you sign the market data agreement.
How fresh is Omega data?
Every Omega level shows when it was last updated, for example "updated 5 min ago", so you always know how old a level is.
Do I need to be an expert?
No. Mentis has plain-English lessons on ICT, SMT and order flow, and Kronos lets you practise on past sessions with no limit.
Can I start my own community?
Yes. Creator Hub is free to start. You can sell indicators and courses, run events and give members a Discord role. Read how it works.
