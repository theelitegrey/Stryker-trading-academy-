Charts, order flow and GEX for futures traders, in a browser.

We build this for people who trade NQ and ES and want to see the order book, the options levels and a place to practise, without installing anything. We change the site most days and write each change down below.

Recently shipped

Dates are IST. Build numbers match our release log.

  1. 4 Nov · Build 862

    Connect your own data: Dhan

    Members can now sign in to Dhan with their own API key and plot that data on the chart. Data only: TradeZoro never places, changes or cancels trades. Alpaca, tastytrade and others use the same sign-in sheet.

  2. 4 Nov · Builds 855 to 859

    Desktop bridge for NinjaTrader 8, MT5 and IBKR

    A small Windows helper pairs with the site with a code and sends prices from your own terminal to your charts. It is unsigned for now, so the download page tells you what Windows will say and how to continue. Tradovate accounts work through NinjaTrader.

  3. 4 Nov · Build 853

    Delta ladder (L2) on the price axis

    Traded delta at each price level, next to the book volume added and pulled there. It sits on the price axis, with the Volume Profile (L2) to its left. A Pro tool. Search “ladder” in Indicators.

  4. 4 Nov · Build 852

    Options flow marks and off-exchange volume levels

    Options flow marks sit on the candles, with only the largest bars drawn. Off-exchange volume levels are free. The marks are Pro.

  5. 4 Nov · Build 851

    Alerts up to 1000, 16 charts, colour flags, earnings calendar

    Alerts go up to 1000 per account. The layout grid holds up to 16 charts. Watchlist rows take colour flags. The US earnings calendar has its own tab and an E marker you can switch on.

  6. 4 Nov · Build 847

    Formula charts

    Type a spread or ratio into the symbol search and it plots as candles. The one below is NQ1! divided by ES1!. It works in the watchlist too.

  7. 4 Nov · Build 845

    Auto chart patterns

    Zigzag pivots, twelve patterns, outlines, labels and necklines, with targets as an option. Free.

  8. 3 Nov · Builds 806 to 811

    Order book heatmap, then the futures order book (L2)

    The heatmap, the DOM ladder and the Book, DOM and Trades panel all went in on the same day. Tools that need the book fill from saved history when you open them.
