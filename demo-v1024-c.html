Questions

Quick answers

What do I get for free?

Chapters 1–10, SPX GEX (same-day view), the Global Monitor, economic calendar and pre-market brief, a journal for up to 20 trades a month, 3 replay sessions a week, and read access to the community.

Which markets do the order flow tools work on?

Supported futures markets such as NQ and ES, and crypto pairs. The liquidity heatmap is BTC and ETH only. You can also connect your own Rithmic data.

Can I place real trades from TradeZoro?

No. Trading on TradeZoro is paper trading, bar replay and backtesting only, so you can practise without real money.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Charts, lessons, GEX and the journal run in a phone browser, and price and indicator alerts can push to your phone.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Create a free account and start with chapters 1–10 and SPX GEX. Upgrade to Pro only if you want the rest.
