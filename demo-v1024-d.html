Fair questions

"But what about…"

Do I have to install anything?

No. TradeZoro runs in your browser, on desktop and on your phone, with the same account on both.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The Free plan needs no card. You get chapters 1–10, SPX GEX, every chart tool and indicator, a journal for up to 20 trades a month and unlimited replay.

I'm new. Is order flow too advanced for me?

The 64 chapters walk you through it step by step, and chapters 1–10 are free. You can practise everything with paper trading and bar replay before you risk anything.

Can I place real trades from TradeZoro?

No. Trading here is practice only: paper trading, bar replay and backtesting. TradeZoro is for learning and practice.

Which markets have order flow?

NQ, ES, YM, GC, SI, CL and crypto. Futures traders can also connect their own data.

Will this make me a profitable trader?

No tool can promise that. TradeZoro gives you the charts, the lessons and a safe place to practise. The work is yours.
