Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. TradeZoro runs in your browser on your phone and your computer, with the same account on both.

What is free?

The Free plan needs no card. You get chapters 1–10, SPX GEX, order flow on NQ (last 24 hours) and crypto, a journal for up to 20 trades a month and 3 replay sessions a week.

Which markets have order flow?

NQ, ES, YM, GC, SI, CL and crypto. Futures traders can also connect their own data.

Can I place real trades here?

No. Trading on TradeZoro is practice only: paper trading, bar replay and backtesting.

I am new. Where do I start?

Start with chapter 1. The chapters build up step by step, and you can practise each idea with replay and paper trading.
