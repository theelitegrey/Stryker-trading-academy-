Desktop app

TradeZoro for Windows Beta

Your TradeZoro desk in its own app. Charts get their own windows for a second monitor, stay on top when you want them to, and alerts arrive as Windows notifications.

Download for Windows Portable version (no install)

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · Free download · All versions and checksums

Why use the app

  • Pop-out chart windowsOpen a chart or a saved layout in its own window and drag it to a second monitor. Each window remembers its size and place.
  • Always on topKeep one chart above your other apps. Turn it on per window with Ctrl+Shift+T.
  • Desktop alertsYour TradeZoro alerts show as Windows notifications, also when the app sits in the tray.
  • Always the current siteThe app opens tradezoro.com, so every site update is in the app the same day. The app itself updates when you restart it, and never during market hours without asking.
  • Keyboard shortcutsCtrl+Shift+N new chart window, Ctrl+R reload, F11 full screen.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Use tradezoro.com in Safari or Chrome. On iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen.

Installing

  1. Open TradeZoro-Setup.exe. It installs for your Windows user only and opens TradeZoro.
  2. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. Your browser may also ask you to Keep the file.
  3. Sign in with your TradeZoro account. Google sign-in works inside the app.
  4. To remove it: Windows Settings, Apps, TradeZoro, Uninstall.

The app shows the same TradeZoro you use in the browser, with the same account and plan. Education only. Not financial advice.
