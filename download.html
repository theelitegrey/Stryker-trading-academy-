Install in three steps

Both apps are in beta, so your device may ask you to confirm once.

  1. Download and open TradeZoro-Setup.exe. It installs for your Windows user only.
  2. The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Sign in with your TradeZoro account. Your charts and settings are already there.

No install? Try the portable version. All versions and checksums.

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (your browser), then tap Install.
  3. Open TradeZoro and sign in. Turn on notifications to get your alerts.

The Play Store version is coming later.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, downloading the apps is free. You sign in with your TradeZoro account, and the plan you have on the web is the same plan in every app.

Is my data synced?

Yes. Your watchlists, chart layouts, settings and paper account live on your account, so a change on one device shows up on the others.

Which brokers can I trade with?

Paper trading for everyone: practise orders on live charts with a paper account. No real money is used.

Is it safe to install?

The files come straight from tradezoro.com. The Windows beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may ask you to confirm with More info, then Run anyway. The Android beta comes from outside the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow this source once.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet, it is coming later. On iPhone and iPad, open tradezoro.com in Safari, then Share and Add to Home Screen.
