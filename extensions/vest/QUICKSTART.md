# Zoro for Vest: quick start

1. Install: Chrome/Edge: unzip, `chrome://extensions`, Developer mode, Load unpacked. Or Tampermonkey: import `zoro-for-vest.user.js`.
2. Open next.vestmarkets.com/trade and sign in to Vest.
3. Read **Before you start**, tick the box and click **I agree**.
4. **Copy** tab: pick your **Master account**. To copy, switch on the follower accounts and choose **1:1** or **Scaled to balance**.
5. **Trade** tab: choose Market or Limit, type the size (or $ risk), and the stop and targets in points. Check the line under them.
6. Click **Buy** or **Sell**, check the summary, and click **Send**.
7. To copy, switch **Copy** ON in the Copy tab. Switch it OFF to stop copying at once.
8. Manage positions with **Breakeven**, **Close all** and **Cancel all**. Check each account in the **Log** tab.
9. Pro levels: paste your link from tradezoro.com/platform-levels in the **Levels** tab and click **Save**.
10. Start with the smallest size. The orders are real. Copies can be late or fail. Education only. Not financial advice.
