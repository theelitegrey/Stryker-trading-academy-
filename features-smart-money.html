Context, honestly framed

This is public-record context, not a signal service — and the page says so. Congressional trades can be filed up to 45 days late; a spouse’s structured note is not a CEO’s cash buy. We teach you to read it properly.

  • Filing lags and ownership types shown on every row
  • Buys and sells always labeled and color-separated
