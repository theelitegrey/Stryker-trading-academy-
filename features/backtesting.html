Kronos · Replay & Backtesting

Practise on past charts, one bar at a time.

Pick a market and a past date and the chart stops there. Step forward, place simulated orders with a stop and a target, tag what you did, then review every session on a dashboard.

Open Kronos

Every figure in these screens is demo data, and result figures are blurred. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

Practice you can actually review

The problem

  • Scrolling back through a chart shows you the answer before you decide.
  • Practice trades on paper are hard to count and easy to forget.
  • Without a log, you can’t tell a good session from a lucky one.

With Kronos

  • The chart only shows what had happened up to the replay clock.
  • Every practice trade is logged with side, size, entry, exit, R and why it closed.
  • Tags, notes and a dashboard across all your sessions.

How it works

Kronos, step by step

Five steps from picking a date to reviewing the session.

  1. 1

    Start a session

    Choose a market, an interval and a past date. Play, step one or ten bars, or jump to the next day.

  2. 2

    Place a simulated trade

    Buy or sell from the chart or the ticket with a stop and a target. When it closes, tag the setup and any mistake.

  3. 3

    Check every trade

    Every closed practice trade in one table, filterable by market, side, session, setup, day and time.

  4. 4

    Read the dashboard

    One dashboard across your sessions: equity curve, winners against losers and how long trades were held.

  5. 5

    Dig into the numbers

    Performance, drawdown and a trade-order simulation, all from your own practice trades.

Questions

Before you start a session

Are the orders real?

No. Kronos is simulated practice. TradeZoro never places real orders.

How are fills worked out?

Market orders fill at the last close plus any slippage you set, gaps fill at the open, and if one bar touches both stop and target the stop counts first. Real fills can differ.

Is the coach AI?

No. It is a fixed set of rules that runs in your browser on your own trades.

Where do sessions live?

In your browser as you go, with a copy on your account so you can resume elsewhere.

Does it predict anything?

No. The simulation re-orders trades you already took. It is not a forecast.
