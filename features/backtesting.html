Demo data. The replay chart.

01 / 09

Bar-by-bar replay

The chart only shows what had happened up to the current bar. Play it at a chosen speed, step one or ten bars, or jump to the next day or the New York open.

How it works

Choose a market, a start date and a window of one week to a few months. Futures, forex and crypto candles are loaded once and kept in your browser, or you can load your own CSV file. Switch timeframes from 1 minute up to weekly, and add a higher-timeframe chart beside it.

Demo data. The practice account and an open position.

02 / 09

Simulated orders

Buy or sell from the chart, the order ticket or the keyboard. Attach a stop and a target, move them, or close part of a position. The practice account tracks balance, equity and open P&L as you step.

How it works

Fills follow simple rules: market orders fill at the last close plus any slippage you set, gaps fill at the open, and if one bar touches both your stop and your target the stop counts first. Commission per contract can be set per session. Real fills can differ.

03 / 09

Every trade, logged

Each closed practice trade is listed with its session, side, size, entry and exit, R-multiple and why it closed. Tag the setup and any mistake, and add a note.

How it works

Filter by market, side, outcome, session, setup, weekday, hour or date. Trades can be exported to CSV or copied into your trade journal.

04 / 09

Backtesting dashboard

One dashboard across all your practice sessions: P&L over time, win and loss breakdowns, performance by side and by trading session, recent sessions and a P&L calendar.

How it works

Sessions are saved in your browser as you go, with a copy on your account so you can resume on another device.

Demo data. Coach findings on the practice session.

05 / 09

The replay coach

Rule-based checks on your own practice trades: losing streaks, overtrading, re-entries straight after a loss, which session costs you, and your most frequent tagged mistake. Each finding comes with a suggestion to consider.

How it works

It is a fixed set of rules that runs in your browser on your own trades. It is not a chatbot and nothing is sent anywhere for it. While you trade it can also show a short nudge, for example after several losses in a row.

Trade-order simulation

Shuffles the order of your practice trades many times to show how much the same trades could have swung in a different sequence.

How it’s calculated

It re-orders the trades you already took. It does not create new trades and it is not a forecast: a small sample, like the one here, says very little.

Questions

Is replay trading real?

No. Orders are simulated against historical candles in your browser. No money and no broker are involved.

Can I see the future candles?

No. The chart stops at the current bar and only moves forward when you step or press play.

Which markets can I replay?

Futures such as NQ, MNQ, ES, MES, YM, RTY, GC and CL, major forex pairs, and BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP against USDT. You can also load your own CSV file of candles.

Is the coach a chatbot?

No. It is a fixed set of rule-based checks that run on your own practice trades in your browser.

Does a good backtest mean the setup works?

No. Practice results are not trading results, and a small sample says very little. Use replay to drill execution and to test whether you follow your own rules.

Which plan includes it?

Free members get 3 backtesting sessions a week. Pro is unlimited, with replay and the full analytics.
