Questions

Where does the data come from?

Crypto candles load from public Binance, Coinbase and Hyperliquid feeds, straight into your browser.

Can I trade from the chart?

No. The workspace is for charting and study. It is not connected to any broker and places no orders.

Which markets are there?

Crypto pairs from those exchanges, such as BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT. To practise futures and forex on past charts, use the replay tool.

Are my drawings saved?

Yes, in the browser you use. Another device or a cleared browser starts with a fresh layout.

Whose charting engine is it?

The workspace is built on Vela, an open-source charting library released under the Apache-2.0 licence.

Which plan includes it?
