Questions

Where does the data come from?

Crypto candles load from public Binance, Coinbase and Hyperliquid feeds. Futures and forex come through our own data server, and futures order flow is built there from every trade. You can also connect your own Rithmic data.

Can I trade from the chart?

Only with demo money. Paper trading fills simulated orders against the chart so you can practise. The workspace is not connected to any broker and places no real orders.

Which markets are there?

Futures such as NQ, ES, YM, RTY, GC, SI and CL with their micros, crypto pairs such as BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT, and major forex pairs.

Are my drawings saved?

Yes. Layouts, drawings, templates and favourites are saved to your account, so they follow you to another device.

Whose charting engine is it?

The workspace is built on Vela, an open-source charting library released under the Apache-2.0 licence.

Which plan includes it?

The charts are free for every plan: every chart type, timeframe, drawing, layout, alert and indicator, paper trading and replay, with no limits. Pro adds two things: the order flow tools and GEX on the charts. CME futures data is 10 minutes delayed (CME rule) on Free and Pro alike; crypto is live. For real-time futures, connect your own Rithmic login, add live futures data for $5/month, or ask us for approval.
