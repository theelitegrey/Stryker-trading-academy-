Indicators and Pine

Built-in studies and Pine scripts

Search one window for moving averages, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, volume profile and more. Pine scripts run in your browser, so you can add the ready-made ones, write your own or load a community script.

  • 24 ready-made Pine scripts: 7 by TradeZoro and 17 community scripts, each marked with its author.
  • A Pine editor with My scripts, saved to your account.
  • Community scripts get a safety check before they run.
  • One Style tab for every indicator: colours, widths and visibility per timeframe.

Market data

Know where every candle comes from

Crypto is live, from public Binance, Coinbase and Hyperliquid feeds. CME futures are 10 minutes delayed by CME rule, unless you connect your own Rithmic login or add the Live Futures Data add-on. Forex comes through our own data server.

  • Rithmic is data only. TradeZoro never places orders, so you keep trading at your broker.
  • A small dot and pill on the chart tell you which feed you are on.
  • Live futures data follows our Market Data Terms.

Questions

Before you open a chart

Is ZoroCharts free?

Yes. Every chart type, interval, indicator, drawing, layout, alert, paper trading and bar replay is on every plan. Pro adds the order flow tools and GEX on the charts. See plans for the details.

Where does the data come from?

Crypto is live from public Binance, Coinbase and Hyperliquid feeds. CME futures are 10 minutes delayed by CME rule, unless you connect your own Rithmic login or add the Live Futures Data add-on. Forex comes through our own data server.

Can I trade from the chart?

Only with demo money. Paper trading fills simulated orders against the chart. TradeZoro never places real orders, and a Rithmic connection is used for data only.

Can I use my own Pine scripts?

Yes. Paste a script into the Pine editor and it runs in your browser. Your scripts are saved to your account. Community scripts get a safety check before they run.

How do I share a chart?

Save the layout, then pick Link only, Public or Community in the Chart Library. Link only copies a link that any signed-in member can open.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The chart is built for touch: pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and the menus open as sheets. Your layouts follow you between phone and desktop.

Whose charting engine is it?

ZoroCharts is built on Vela, an open-source charting library released under the Apache-2.0 licence, with our own tools on top.
