ZoroCharts · Charting

The whole charting desk, in your browser.

Futures, crypto and forex charts with indicators, Pine scripts, drawings and up to 16 charts on one screen. Order flow, GEX levels, bar replay, paper trading and alerts sit on the same chart, on your phone and your desktop.

Open ZoroCharts

The charts are free on every plan. Screens are the real tool on sample data. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

One chart instead of a stack of apps

The problem

  • Charts in one app, order flow in another and options levels in a spreadsheet.
  • Layouts, drawings and scripts stuck on one computer.
  • Practising means a separate replay tool and a separate demo account.

With ZoroCharts

  • Indicators, Pine scripts, order flow and GEX levels on the same chart.
  • Layouts, drawings, templates and scripts saved to your account, on any device.
  • Bar replay and paper trading with demo money, built into the chart.

How it works

ZoroCharts, step by step

Five steps from an empty chart to a saved, shareable layout.

  1. 1

    Pick a market

    Search futures, crypto or forex and choose an interval, from seconds and minutes to months.

  2. 2

    Add indicators and Pine scripts

    One window holds the built-in studies, the TradeZoro scripts and Pine scripts, each with its own inputs and style.

  3. 3

    Draw on it

    Trend lines, boxes, Fibonacci tools, patterns and the long and short position tool, with a magnet that snaps to the candle.

  4. 4

    Lay out up to 16 charts

    Pick from 15 multi-chart arrangements and link the charts so symbol, interval, crosshair and time move together.

  5. 5

    Save and share

    Save the layout to your account, then keep it private, share it by link, publish it to the Chart Library or to your community.

Questions

Before you open a chart

Is ZoroCharts free?

Yes. Every chart type, interval, indicator, drawing, layout, alert, paper trading and bar replay is on every plan. Order flow tools and GEX on the charts are free too.

Can I trade from the chart?

Only with demo money. Paper trading fills simulated orders against the chart. TradeZoro never places real orders.

Can I use my own Pine scripts?

Yes. Paste a script into the Pine editor and it runs in your browser. Your scripts are saved to your account. Community scripts get a safety check before they run.

How do I share a chart?

Save the layout, then pick Link only, Public or Community in the Chart Library. Link only copies a link that any signed-in member can open.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The chart is built for touch: pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and the menus open as sheets. Your layouts follow you between phone and desktop.

Whose charting engine is it?

ZoroCharts is built on an open-source charting library released under the Apache-2.0 licence, with our own tools on top. The library is credited in the chart’s Credits panel.
