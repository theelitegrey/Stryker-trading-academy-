Questions

Who can see my posts?

Posts on the Trading Floor are visible to every signed-in student.

Can I post on every plan?

Everyone signed in can read the floor. Posting, reacting, replying and direct messages are part of Pro; the floor shows a note with a link to the plans if yours is read-only.

How do referrals work?

Anyone who signs up through your link is credited to you automatically, and you earn points for every trader who joins. Points are shown on your Invite & earn page.

Are posts trade signals?

No. Posts are members’ own notes and questions, shared to learn the method.
