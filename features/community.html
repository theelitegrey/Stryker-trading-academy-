Questions

Before you start

Who can see my posts?

Posts on the Trading Floor are visible to every signed-in member.

Can I post on every plan?

Everyone signed in can read the floor. Posting, reacting, replying and direct messages depend on your plan; the floor shows a note if yours is read-only.

How do referrals work?

Anyone who signs up through your link is credited to you automatically, and you earn points. Points show on your Invite & earn page.

Are posts trade signals?

No. Posts are members’ own notes and questions, shared to learn the method.
