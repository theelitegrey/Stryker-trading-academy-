What you can offer

Everything your members need, under your name

Indicators

Publish an indicator that runs on ZoroCharts

Upload a Pine script, check it compiles, and choose who gets it: a plan, a free claim, an invite code or one email at a time. Members add it to their chart in one click.

  • Source stays hidden. Each member gets their own tagged copy.
  • Access is checked every time a chart loads it, so ending a plan ends access.
  • Versions, settings notes, screenshots and 7-day usage.
Courses

Teach with courses people can finish

Build modules and lessons from video, text, PDF and links. Add a quiz, set a drip date, and members see their own progress and where to resume.

  • Video from YouTube, Vimeo or Loom links.
  • PDFs open in a viewer with the member's email as a moving watermark.
  • Lock a lesson to a plan, a date or the lesson before it.
Community

Keep the conversation in your room

A members-only feed for posts and comments, an events calendar with RSVPs, and a leaderboard that shows who is most active. You can pin posts and remove anything that breaks your rules.

  • Feed with pinned posts and comments.
  • Events with a time, a link and a going count.
  • Leaderboard counts activity only. It is not a trading ranking.
Discord

Discord roles that follow the plan

Connect your Discord server once and pick a role for each plan. Members link their Discord, get the role, and lose it when their membership ends. No manual role handing.

  • TradeZoro sees only the member's Discord name and ID.
  • Members can unlink any time.
  • You keep running your server your way.

Questions

Before you start

What does it cost?

Starting is free. Founding Creators pay 0% TradeZoro platform fee during launch. When you sell a paid plan, Stripe's standard card fees apply. We will tell creators before anything changes.

How do I get paid?

Members pay you directly through Stripe, and payouts go to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule. Stripe is only available in some countries, and the setup screen shows whether yours is covered. If it is not, free and invite-only communities still work.

Who handles refunds?

You do. You are an independent creator, so you set a refund rule that shows on your page and you answer your members. The Creator Rules cover what you must tell them.

How is my indicator code protected?

It is hidden and traceable, not unbreakable. Members run it on their chart without seeing the source, and each delivered copy carries a hidden mark that can be traced to the account that loaded it. No protection is perfect, so only publish what you can live with being copied.

How do Discord roles work?

You connect your Discord server and choose a role for each plan. Members link their Discord, get the role, and lose it when their membership ends.

Can I start free?

Yes. Free plans, invite links and member import work today, and you can switch to paid plans later. Each account can run up to 3 communities.

What can't I sell or post?

No promised profits, income or win rates, no fake proof or testimonials, no copied or leaked material, and no trading bots or signals run through TradeZoro. Read the full Creator Rules before you start.

Does TradeZoro vouch for creators?

No. Every community is run by an independent creator, and TradeZoro does not verify creators or their claims. Members can report a community from its page.
