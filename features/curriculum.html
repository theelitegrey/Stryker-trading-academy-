Questions

Before you start

Is the curriculum free?

Yes. All 64 chapters, including both specialist tracks, are free with an account. No card required.

Do I have to go in order?

The chapters are built to be taken in order, because each one assumes the ones before it. You can open any chapter from the list.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Every chapter you complete is saved to your account and shows on your dashboard on any device.

Is it video?

No. The chapters are written lessons with chart diagrams.

Is this financial advice?

No. The curriculum teaches methods many traders use, for study. It does not tell you what to trade.
