Global Monitor · Market context

The market’s weather report, on one screen

Before you pick a trade you need the context: what is moving, where the risk tone sits, what is on the calendar. The Global Monitor is the one-screen check before every session.

What’s on the Global Monitor

Six tabs on one screen: Markets, Map, Wire, Risk, Briefing (a rule-based summary, not AI) and Streams.

The Wire headline feed.

01 / 05

The Wire

Headlines collected from third-party feeds, searchable by headline, country and source, so the context reaches you without twelve open tabs.

How it works

Chips filter the feed by severity; the search box matches headlines, countries and sources.

Market signals and asset tables.

02 / 05

Markets

Market signals, then the instruments that set the tone, grouped on one screen.

How it works

Risk tone, sector breadth, rotation leader, curve and volatility at the top; indices, futures, safe havens, FX, energy, defense stocks and crypto below. Sector rotation and movers follow.

Financial map.

03 / 05

Financial map

Monetary policy and market-stress stories by country, with central banks marked, plus a financial newswire filtered by severity.

How it works

Hover a central bank for its next high-impact release; hover a dot for the stories behind it.

DEFCON status and disease outbreaks.

04 / 05

Risk

DEFCON status, labelled on the page as a third-party estimate, beside disease outbreaks ranked by severity from WHO Disease Outbreak News.

How it works

The page names the source under each panel.

Prediction markets.

05 / 05

Prediction markets

Trending prediction markets, searchable, as another read on what the crowd is weighing. Prices are the crowd’s odds, not forecasts.

How it works

Ranked by 24h volume, via Polymarket. Shown for context only; not a forecast.

Questions

What is the Global Monitor for?

It is the one-screen context check before a session: events, headlines, markets, risk and the calendar in one place.

Where does the data come from?

Third-party sources, named on each panel (for example Polymarket, the Forex Factory feed and WHO Disease Outbreak News), collected into one page. For context only.

Is it part of the Terminal?

Yes. It sits beside Charts and the Smart Money desk in the Terminal section of your dashboard.
