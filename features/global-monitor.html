What’s on the monitor

Five panels, one screen

    The Wire

    Headlines from third-party feeds, searchable by headline, country and source.

Questions

Before you open the monitor

What is it for?

It is the one-screen context check before a session: events, headlines, markets, risk and the calendar in one place.

Where does the data come from?

Third-party sources, named on each panel (for example Polymarket, the Forex Factory feed and WHO Disease Outbreak News). For context only.

Is the briefing AI?

No. It is a rule-based summary.

Where is it?

In the Terminal section of your dashboard, beside ZoroCharts and the Smart Money desk.
