Liquidity Master.

Liquidity Master

Maps the highs, lows and equal levels that traders watch as liquidity, so the chart shows them at a glance.

How it works

Taught in the curriculum’s liquidity chapters: buy-side and sell-side liquidity, and what a sweep of it looks like.

SMT Divergence Pro, NQ against ES.

02 / 06

SMT Divergence Pro

Compares correlated instruments and marks SMT divergence on the chart when one makes a new high or low and the other does not.

How it works

One market makes a new high or low and the correlated one does not confirm: the crack the SMT chapters teach you to read.

IFVG Pro.

03 / 06

IFVG Pro

Marks inversion fair value gaps: FVGs that price has traded through, which some traders then treat as support or resistance.

How it works

A gap that price breaks through can switch roles; IFVG Pro marks the inverted zone and the retest of it.

FVG Relay.

04 / 06

FVG Relay

Follows chains of fair value gaps across a move, showing which price has respected and which it has traded through.

How it works

Each gap is drawn with its consequent-encroachment line and tracked as price returns to it.

HTF PO3 Lens.

05 / 06

HTF PO3 Lens

Projects higher-timeframe Power of Three structure onto your execution timeframe.

How it works

Higher-timeframe candles sit beside your chart with their open extended across it, read as accumulation, manipulation and distribution.
