Private Indicators · on ZoroCharts

The concepts you learn, marked on the chart.

Our indicators automate one idea from the curriculum each: liquidity, SMT divergence, fair value gaps, sweeps and higher-timeframe candles. Add them in ZoroCharts in one click, and every one has its own guide.

Open TradeZoro indicatorsRead the guide

Indicators mark concepts on the chart; they are not trade signals. Screens are the real tool on sample data. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

Stop redrawing the same levels every morning

The problem

  • Marking session highs, lows and gaps by hand takes time before every session.
  • Two traders mark the same concept two different ways.
  • A script you cannot read is a black box you cannot learn from.

With TradeZoro indicators

  • Each indicator marks one concept from the curriculum the same way, every time.
  • Every indicator has a guide page: what it marks, how it works and its settings.
  • They run in ZoroCharts next to your own drawings, on any device.

How it works

TradeZoro indicators, step by step

Four steps from the library to a marked-up chart.

  1. 1

    Browse the library

    The Indicators page lists every indicator by group, with what it shows and a link to its guide.

  2. 2

    Add it in ZoroCharts

    Open the Indicators window, pick the TradeZoro group and click an indicator. No other account needed.

  3. 3

    Read the marks

    The indicator draws its levels, boxes and labels on the chart. Change inputs and colours in its settings.

  4. 4

    Learn what it marks

    Each guide explains the concept, how the script finds it and what every setting does.

Concept scripts 7

  • Liquidity MasterSession, previous day / week / month, swing and candle liquidity per timeframe, HTF FVGs, REQH / REQL and draw-on-liquidity tables.
  • SMT Divergence ProSMT divergence vs correlated futures (auto-paired: NQ vs ES), chart TF + higher TFs.
  • IFVG Pro+Liquidity (BSL / SSL from higher-TF swings and candles), sweep, candidate FVG and inversion (IFVG) boxes, risk / reward box.
  • FVG RelayHigher-timeframe FVGs on your chart; tapped and respected gaps chain as Relay 1, 2, 3; liquidity and dashboard.
  • HTF PO3 LensUp to five higher-timeframe candles drawn right of the last bar, with HTF FVGs, sweeps and a countdown to the close.

Options and GEX 8

  • GEX LevelsCall wall, put wall, zero gamma, the implied range and prior-session levels from Omega.
  • GEX HeatmapNet GEX at every strike through the session, drawn behind the candles.
  • Expected MoveOptions-implied expected move (ATM straddle) for 0DTE, 1DTE, weekly and monthly.
  • ZoroTideOptions money flow: net call premium (green) vs net put premium (red) since the open, in a lower pane.
  • Options Flow MarksBubbles on the candles where call (green) or put (red) premium was large, sized by premium; hover or tap for details. Per-bar totals, not single trades.
  • Delta ExposureDealer delta exposure by strike on the price axis, with the 3 largest strikes as lines. All expiries, 0DTE or next expiry.
  • Vanna ExposureDealer vanna exposure by strike on the price axis, with the 3 largest strikes as lines. All expiries, 0DTE or next expiry.
  • Charm ExposureDealer charm exposure by strike on the price axis, with the 3 largest strikes as lines. All expiries, 0DTE or next expiry.

Market context 6

  • Off-exchange VolumeDaily off-exchange share of volume in a lower pane, from public regulatory data, updated daily.

Questions

Before you add an indicator

Where do I use them?

In ZoroCharts. Open the Indicators window, pick the TradeZoro group and add one. No other account is needed.

Are these buy and sell signals?

No. Each script marks a concept the curriculum teaches, so you learn why a level matters and then let the script do the marking.

Can I change how they look?

Yes. Every indicator has inputs and a Style tab for colours, widths and visibility per timeframe.

Where can I read how one works?

Every indicator has its own guide page in the indicator library, with what it marks and every setting.

Can I use them outside ZoroCharts?

If your plan includes it, you can request access on the Indicator access page. Access is set up by hand, so it can take a little time.
