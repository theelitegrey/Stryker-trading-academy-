Questions

Before you log a trade

Is the coach AI?

No. It is a fixed set of rules that checks your own entries in your browser.

Do my trades leave my account?

Not for the coach. The checks run on your device from your own journal.

Is the demo data mine?

No. The screenshots use the built-in demo data. You can remove it any time.

Does it promise results?

No. It measures what you logged. Education only.
