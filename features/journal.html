Demo data. The coach tab.

06 / 09

The coach

Rule-based checks on your own journal: expectancy, drawdown, edge by setup and session, early exits, trading after losses, overtrading, sizing and fees. Each finding is tagged fix first, leak, strength or pattern.

How it works

The checks run in your browser on your own entries. It is a fixed set of rules, not a chatbot, and your trades are not sent anywhere for it.

Demo data. Simulation output, not a prediction.

09 / 09

Evaluation simulator

Enter your firm’s rules and the simulator replays the evaluation many times from your own win rate and R-multiples, then shows what share of runs reached a payout at each position size.

How it works

It is a simulation of your past results against the rules, not a prediction. The panel says so itself: real losses cluster, so read the figure as an optimistic bound.

Questions

Where do the numbers come from?

From the trades you log or import. The screenshots on this page use the journal’s built-in demo data.

Is the coach a chatbot?

No. It is a fixed set of rule-based checks that run on your own journal entries in your browser.

Can I import trades?

Yes. Import a CSV from your broker or another journal; the columns are matched for you and you can correct the mapping before anything is saved. Payouts and expenses can be imported too.

Does the simulator predict whether I will pass?

No. It replays your past results against your firm’s rules. It is a simulation, not a prediction, and it reads as an optimistic bound.

Can I share my results?

Yes. The journal can draw a P&L card on your device and download it as an image. Nothing is uploaded.
