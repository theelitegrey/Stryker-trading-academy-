Questions

When are sessions held?

Sessions are scheduled by the desk and shown on the page with a countdown. Times appear in your own timezone.

Which plan do I need?

The session room with chat is part of Elite. The replay library is part of Pro. Free members can see the schedule.

Are sessions trade signals?

No. Sessions show how the concepts are applied to a market, to teach the method.
