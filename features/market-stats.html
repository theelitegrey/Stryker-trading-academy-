The reports

Reports on the regular session

  1. 1
    Gap fill

    How often an opening gap traded back to the prior close, how fast, by gap size and weekday.

  2. 2
    Opening candle continuation

    How often the first 5, 15 or 30 minute candle closed on the same side as the session.

  3. 3
    Inside and outside days

    Days that stayed inside, or took out both sides of, the day before, and what the next session did.

  4. 4
    Previous day range

    Where the session opened against yesterday’s range, and where it closed.

  5. 5
    Average daily range

    How much of the 20-day average range the session used, and when.

Built to be honest

A small sample should look small

Regular session, 09:30 to 16:00 New York, up to three years of 5-minute history.

N on every number

Each card shows how many sessions it covers. Under 30 it carries a low-sample tag; under 10 the number is withheld.

95% interval

A Wilson interval shows how much room the estimate has, plus a check that the first and second half of the sessions agree.

See the sessions

Tap a number to list the matching sessions, then open one on ZoroCharts with prior day levels, gap and range lines drawn.
