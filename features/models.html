Trading Models · Mentis

Complete models, not lone setups.

A library of 11 written playbooks: the context to look for, the trigger to wait for, where the idea is wrong and how it is managed after entry. Every step documented, with a checklist to work through.

Open Trading Models

Models are methods to study, not trade signals. Walkthrough charts are illustrative. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

A setup is not a plan

The problem

  • A screenshot of a setup leaves out when it is valid and where it is wrong.
  • Rules live in your head and change from day to day.
  • Two traders read the same pattern two different ways.

With Trading Models

  • Each model is written to one standard: context, trigger, invalidation, management.
  • A numbered checklist you tick off as you learn the model.
  • A diagram and an illustrative walkthrough for every step.

How it works

Trading Models, step by step

Five steps from the library to a model you can run from a checklist.

  1. 1

    Start with the process

    The same five steps sit at the top of the library: prep, apply the rules, confirm, size the risk, journal it.

  2. 2

    Browse the library

    Filter the cards by category: Opening & Session, Liquidity & Reversal, Imbalance / FVG or Mean Reversion.

  3. 3

    Read one model

    Each model has a diagram, a written walkthrough, its entry criteria and its stop. The stop is part of the model.

  4. 4

    Step through an example

    Step through the setup one stage at a time on an illustrative chart. The page labels it an illustrative example.

  5. 5

    Work the checklist

    Every model ends in a numbered checklist you tick off as you learn it, with a reset button.

Opening & Session 4

  • ORB StrategyA 5-minute opening range breakout on NQ or ES, targeting pre-marked liquidity with a tight 15-20 point stop.
  • Judas Swing ModelThe false opening move that traps early participants before the real session direction develops — with the exact confirmation rules to avoid getting caught by it yourself.
  • ICT Silver BulletA one-hour window, one sweep of the recent range, and the first fair value gap against it: a time-filtered FVG entry on NQ with fixed stop and target rules.
  • Initial Balance + 80% RuleA Market Profile model: when NQ opens outside yesterday's value area and is accepted back inside it, trade the rotation to the far side of value, filtered by the first-hour initial balance.

Liquidity & Reversal 3

  • ICT 2022 ModelThe most widely taught ICT entry sequence: liquidity sweep, Market Structure Shift with displacement, and entry at the resulting FVG for a minimum 1:3 risk-reward.
  • CRT — Candle Range TheoryOne candle sets a range, the next sweeps beyond it, then price reverses back inside — trade the reversal, confirmed with MSS on a lower timeframe.
  • Turtle SoupThe Connors & Raschke failed-breakout setup from Street Smarts (1995), adapted to 5-minute NQ: buy back above a broken 20-bar low (or sell below a 20-bar high) with a tight stop.

Imbalance / FVG 3

  • 15M FVG StrategyCatching continuation and reversal trades off 15-minute fair value gaps, confirmed with MSS/CHoCH and SMT before entry.
  • IFVG ModelSweep the session or daily high/low, confirm with SMT, mark a 5+ point IFVG, and target the opposite swing low.
  • ICT UnicornA setup ICT traders call the Unicorn: after a sweep and a market structure shift, enter where a breaker block and a fair value gap overlap, with the stop beyond the sweep.

Mean Reversion 1

  • VWAP Reversion & ReclaimTwo mechanical VWAP setups on NQ: fade a stretch beyond the 2σ band back to VWAP, or trade a clean reclaim of VWAP towards the 1σ band, with a slope filter for trend days.

Questions

Before you start

What is a trading model?

A complete written method: the context to look for, the trigger, where the idea is wrong, and how the trade is managed after entry.

Are the walkthrough charts real trades?

No. The walkthroughs use illustrative candles drawn to teach the pattern. The page says so.

Are models trade signals?

No. Models are methods to study. They do not tell you what to trade.

Will more models be added?

New models may be added to the library over time.

Does TradeZoro place trades from a model?

No. TradeZoro never places real orders. Practise in paper trading and bar replay.
