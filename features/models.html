The anatomy

Context, trigger, invalidation, management

Each model is written to the same standard, so two students read the same setup the same way. Open any model and you find the same parts in the same order.

  • Context: the conditions that must exist first.
  • Trigger: what you wait for before acting.
  • Invalidation: where the idea is wrong, decided before entry.
  • Management: how the trade is handled after entry.

Questions

Before you start

What is a trading model?

A complete written method that traders use: the context to look for, the trigger, where the idea is wrong, and how the trade is managed after entry.

Are the walkthrough charts real trades?

No. The walkthroughs use illustrative candles drawn to teach the pattern. The page says so.

Are models trade signals?

No. Models are methods to study. They do not tell you what to trade.

Will more models be added?

New models may be added to the library over time.

Does TradeZoro place trades from a model?

No. TradeZoro never places real orders. Practice in paper trading and bar replay.
