Order Flow · ZoroCharts

See who is trading at every price.

Footprint, delta, CVD, big trades, absorption and the order book heatmap, built from real trades and drawn on the same chart you mark up. Futures (NQ, ES, YM, GC, SI, CL and their micros) and crypto.

Why it exists

A candle hides who pushed it

The problem

  • A candle shows where price went, not who pushed it there.
  • A volume bar totals the bar, so buying and selling at each price stay hidden.
  • Big orders and absorbed pushes sit inside a normal-looking candle.

With ZoroCharts

  • Footprint splits every candle into prices, with the volume bought and sold at each one.
  • Delta and CVD show whether buyers or sellers led, bar by bar and over the session.
  • Big trades, absorption and the book heatmap mark where size showed up.

How it works

ZoroCharts, step by step

Five steps from a plain chart to reading the tape on it.

  1. 1

    Add Footprint to the chart

    Open the Indicators window, go to the Order Flow group and add Footprint. Every candle splits into prices with the volume sold and bought at each one; the busiest price is boxed.

  2. 2

    Read delta at each price

    The delta profile shows buyers or sellers row by row at each price, with the largest buy and sell rows tagged.

  3. 3

    Check CVD for divergence

    Cumulative volume delta runs in its own pane, with a line drawn when price and CVD disagree.

  4. 4

    See the resting orders

    The order book heatmap draws resting orders at every price behind the candles, and tags large orders that were pulled or filled.

  5. 5

    Mark where size traded

    Big Trades adds a bubble wherever one large order traded, sized by the order, buyers in green and sellers in red.

Footprint and delta 11

  • FootprintEvery candle split by price, with the volume bought and sold at each one. The busiest price is boxed. Crypto charts; futures use Footprint (L2).
  • Footprint (L2)Traded volume at each price with the resting bid and ask size at bar close beside it. Unusually large resting size is boxed.
  • Footprint PatternsMarks six footprint patterns on any chart style: absorption, exhaustion, stacked imbalances, unfinished auctions, delta divergence and trapped traders.
  • Bar StatsA table under the candles on any chart style: total volume, delta, delta %, buy, sell and trades for every bar, heat-mapped.
  • DeltaBuying minus selling per bar, with 14 looks from a session profile to delta bars and bubbles. Crypto charts; futures use Delta (L2).
  • Delta (L2)Buying minus selling per bar, plus how much resting size was added or pulled from the book over the same bar.
  • Delta Ladder (L2)Delta at each price on the price axis: traded buy and sell, plus a thin bar for book size added or pulled. Added with Delta (L2).
  • Delta Profile (L2)Delta at each price over the last 5 to 60 minutes, a range you pick, or the session: buying to the right, selling to the left.
  • CVDCumulative volume delta from real trades: the running total of buying against selling. Crypto charts; futures use Cumulative Delta (L2).
  • Cumulative Delta (L2)The running total of trade delta and book delta, as candles or a line, with a reset each session.
  • Estimated CVDA delta estimate worked out from the candles, for charts that have no trade data.

Profiles and VWAP 7

  • Volume ProfileVolume by price for the session or a swing, with the point of control and naked POCs.
  • Volume Profile (L2)The session profile of traded volume next to the average resting size at each price.
  • Swing VWAP + Profile (L2)For every swing leg, high to low and low to high: an anchored VWAP and a volume profile with POC, VAH and VAL. New swings are picked up on their own.
  • VWAPVolume-weighted average price from real trades, with one and two standard-deviation bands.
  • Market ProfileTPO letters or blocks per session, with initial balance, value area and single prints.
  • Effort vs ResultA pane that compares volume with range and flags high-effort, low-result bars.
  • Liquidity Trend HeatmapHeat zones from volume at price, with the point of control and a trend baseline, built from the chart volume.

Big trades and stops 7

  • Big TradesBubbles for the largest trades, buyers in green and sellers in red. Crypto charts; futures use Big Trades (L2).
  • Big Trades (L2)Big orders marked by what the book did: hit a wall that held, consumed the resting size, or found it pulled.
  • Volume BubbleBubbles on high-volume bars, built from the chart volume, so heavy bars stand out.
  • AbsorptionFlags bars where heavy aggressive volume hit a price and price did not move.
  • Imbalance ZonesStacked buy or sell imbalances drawn as zones that extend until price trades through them.
  • Stop RunsMarks a bar that pokes past a recent swing on a volume spike and closes back inside.
  • Stop Runs (L2)Stop runs counted only when resting liquidity on that side was pulled or consumed through several levels.

Order book 14

  • Order book heatmapResting orders at every price as a heatmap behind the candles, in four looks: Midnight, Zoro, Classic and Split.
  • Liquidity MapThe order book heatmap with trade bubbles on top, so you see what rested and what traded.
  • Depth HeatmapResting orders from soft blue to red walls, each level a steady bar, with trade balls, the book at the right edge and volume bars.
  • DOM ladderA price ladder beside the chart: bids, asks and the volume bought and sold at each price.
  • Book · DOM · Trades panelOne panel with three tabs: the book, the DOM ladder and the trade tape. Paper orders from the ladder are simulated.
  • Liquidity WallsMarks large resting orders that stay in the book, and tags them when they are pulled or consumed.
  • Book ImbalanceShows when the bid side or the ask side of the book is heavier.
  • Book SpeedHow fast the book is changing, shown as a pane under the chart.
  • Liquidity BalanceResting size on the bid side against the ask side over time.
  • Refill DetectorSpots a price level whose resting orders are refilled after they trade.
  • Volatility RegimeShows whether the market is quiet or fast compared with its own recent past.
  • Large Resting OrdersLists and marks the biggest orders waiting in the book.
  • Absorption & Iceberg TrackerTracks prices that keep absorbing volume and hidden-size hints.
  • Wall EventsMarks what happened to each big resting order on the heatmap: eaten, held, or pulled before price got there.

Crypto derivatives 3

  • LiquidationsForced closes on crypto perpetuals as bubbles at their price and time, longs and shorts in two colours, plus a per-bar total. Exchanges send only part of them, so totals are a lower bound.
  • Open Interest (crypto)Open interest summed across the exchanges you pick, with the change per bar coloured against price.
  • Funding and long/short ratioFunding rate per bar with the rate now and the countdown on each exchange, and the exchange-published long/short account ratio.

Questions

Before you add a tool

Where does the data come from?

Futures order flow is built on our server from every trade and the side that started it. Crypto order flow comes from public exchange trades.

Which markets have order flow?

NQ, ES, YM, GC, SI and CL with their micro contracts, and crypto pairs.

Can I practise on past sessions?

Yes. Every order flow tool works in bar replay and uses only the trades up to the replay clock.

Which plan includes it?

Every plan: order flow tools are free for every signed-in member. On futures, the live order book (L2) is a separate data feed because exchange rules set it; crypto is live for everyone.

Does it place orders?

No. TradeZoro never places real orders. Paper trading is simulated.
