How to read a filing

Context, honestly framed. A spouse’s structured note is not a CEO’s cash buy. Tap a step, or let it play.

Filings are context, not signals. They do not say what price will do.

Questions

Is this a signal service?

No. It is public-record context, and the page says so. Use it to understand who is trading what, not as a trade trigger.

Where does the data come from?

From official records (SEC EDGAR, Senate eFD and the House Clerk), via LuxAlgo’s public-domain dataset parsed from those filings. Rows link to the official filing they came from.

Why are some congressional trades filed weeks later?

Members of Congress are due to file within 45 days of a trade, and some file later. Each row shows both the trade date and the filing date so you can see the gap.

Can I search for one stock or one person?

Yes. Search by politician, executive, ticker or company, and filter to buys, sells or the full list.
