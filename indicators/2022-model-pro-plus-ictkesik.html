2022 Model Pro+ | ICTKESIK

IICTKESIK CommunityFree Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

ICT 2022-model toolkit: liquidity sweeps, market structure shifts, FVGs, breakers, SMT, key levels, killzones and graded setups.

Community script. Script by ICTKESIK. No licence is stated in the script. TradeZoro did not write it. ZoroCharts runs it as the author published it, and its source code is not shown here.

Overview

This community script maps the steps of the ICT 2022 model on the chart. It marks liquidity sweeps with a $$$ tag, the market structure shift (MSS) that follows, the displacement fair value gap, and breaker blocks. It also draws higher-timeframe FVGs, previous day and week highs and lows, session highs and lows, and vertical time lines for midnight, 8:30 and 9:30 New York.

When the steps line up, it draws a setup with entry, stop and two targets, optional risk/reward boxes, a grade, and a checklist table that ticks off each condition.

How it works

Swings are built from pivots (Swing Length 3 by default). A sweep is a wick beyond a recent swing within a set number of bars; an MSS is a close (or wick) through the opposite pivot within a waiting window. The FVG and breaker come from the displacement leg.

SMT divergence compares the chart with a correlated symbol that it picks automatically for common futures, FX, metals and crypto pairs, or one you choose. Higher-timeframe FVGs come from two timeframes chosen from the chart interval, or set manually.

Each setup gets a grade from how many items it meets (SMT, FVG, premium/discount zone, session). Only setups at or above Min Grade are shown, by default inside killzones only.

How to use it

  • Use the checklist to see which parts of the model are present on the current setup.
  • Some traders study the sweep, MSS and FVG sequence to learn how the model is put together.
  • Watch the higher-timeframe FVGs and previous day levels as reference areas.
  • Filter by bias or grade to see fewer, stricter setups.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

Members can create an alert on its conditions (for example "ICTKESIK Setup Formed", "ICTKESIK Entry Filled", "ICTKESIK Stop Hit", "SMT Divergence") from the chart's alert dialog; alerts from indicators run while ZoroCharts is open in the browser.

Notes and limits

  • Times and killzones follow New York time.
  • SMT needs data for the correlated symbol.
  • Entry, stop and target lines are the script's own rules, not advice; review them on past data before relying on them.
  • It has many drawings; on small screens you may want to turn some modules off.
