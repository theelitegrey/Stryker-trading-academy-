Charm Exposure

TZTradeZoro TradeZoroPro Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

Draws dealer charm exposure (CEX) by strike as bars on the price axis, with the 3 largest strikes as lines. All expiries, 0DTE or the next expiry.

Overview

Charm Exposure shows, strike by strike, how much the options' delta exposure changes with one day of time passing, all else equal. Bars grow from the right edge at each strike's price, green for positive and red for negative.

The 3 strikes with the largest charm exposure are drawn as lines with a tag such as "CEX 1 · 6900 · -$310M". With the 0DTE setting it shows only today's expiry, where time decay is fastest. The status line shows the net total per day and when the data was last updated.

How it works

Charm is the change in an option's delta as time passes, from the Black-Scholes model at the chain's spot and the time left to the 16:00 ET close of each expiry, expressed per calendar day. Each option's implied volatility is used, or where the chain has none, the volatility that prices it at its bid/ask mid.

Charm exposure = charm × open interest × 100 × spot (dollars of delta per day). Same sign convention as our GEX: dealers are assumed long the calls and short the puts, so calls count plus and puts minus. Each bar is the sum of every expiry in the chosen set, per strike within 6% of spot, in millions of dollars. These are model estimates from open interest, not real dealer positions.

How to use it

  • Some traders look at charm on 0DTE to see where time decay changes delta the most during the day.
  • Compare the All expiries view with 0DTE to see how much of the total comes from today's expiry.
  • Some traders watch the largest charm strikes into the afternoon.
  • Turn off the bars and keep only the 3 lines for a cleaner chart.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Notes and limits

  • SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM, GLD and USO, futures mapped from them (ES, NQ, RTY, GC, CL), and the top US stocks with options. Crypto and forex show a short note instead.
  • Index and ETF data is updated every 2 minutes during US cash hours, stocks every 15 minutes; the status line says when (for example "updated 3 min ago").
  • 0DTE shows a note on days when the symbol has no expiry today. Nothing is drawn in bar replay.
