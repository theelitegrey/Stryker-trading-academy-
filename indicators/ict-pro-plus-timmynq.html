ICT Pro+ | TimmyNQ [TakingProphets]

TTTimmyNQ (TakingProphets) CommunityFree Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

ICT toolkit: inversion FVGs, CISD lines, HTF gaps, weekly, daily and session highs and lows, session boxes and SMT against a paired symbol.

Community script. Script by TimmyNQ (TakingProphets). No licence is stated in the script. TradeZoro did not write it. ZoroCharts runs it as the author published it, and its source code is not shown here.

Overview

This community script gathers ICT reference points in one overlay. It draws inversion fair value gaps after liquidity sweeps, CISD lines, and the nearest unmitigated higher-timeframe gap on each side. It marks previous week and day highs and lows, Asia and London highs and lows, the all-time high, and the 8:30 AM New York open.

It also shades Asia, London, NY AM and NY PM sessions, and draws SMT divergence lines between the chart and a correlated symbol.

How it works

After a sweep, the most recent qualifying FVG that price closes through is drawn as an inversion box; pending gaps can be shown first and are removed if they never invert. The open of the delivering leg is tracked, and a close back through it prints a CISD line. A CISD whose implied stop is broken is removed.

Higher-timeframe gaps come from two timeframes chosen from the chart interval. Levels close to each other (within 8 ticks by default) share one label.

SMT pairs are automatic for NQ-ES, MNQ-MES, YM-ES, MYM-MES, GC-SI and MGC-SIL, or manual. A sensitivity setting sets swing size for the three-point pattern, and an optional mode adds gap divergence.

How to use it

  • Use the level labels to see where key highs and lows sit before the session opens.
  • Some traders watch inversion FVGs and CISDs that form after a sweep of a marked level.
  • Use session boxes to see which session made the high or low of the day.
  • Watch SMT lines when the chart and its paired market disagree at a swing.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Notes and limits

  • Session times are New York time; session labels show on 15-minute and lower charts.
  • SMT needs data for the correlated symbol and is made for the futures and metal pairs listed.
  • The script has no alert conditions.
