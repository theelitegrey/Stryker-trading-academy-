iFVG Ultimate+ Deluxe | DodgysDD

DDodgysDD CommunityFree Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

Inversion FVG model with graded setups, sweeps, equal highs and lows, sessions, HTF FVGs, SMT, NWOGs, delta coloring and a checklist.

Community script. Script by DodgysDD. No licence is stated in the script. TradeZoro did not write it. ZoroCharts runs it as the author published it, and its source code is not shown here.

Overview

This community script builds setups around inversion fair value gaps (IFVGs): gaps that price closes through and that then act from the other side. Each setup gets a grade, entry markers, a stop, break-even and two targets, with an optional contract-size suggestion from a risk amount.

Around the setups it can draw liquidity sweeps, relative equal highs and lows, session highs and lows and boxes, previous day and week levels, midnight, 8:30 and 9:30 opens, higher-timeframe FVGs, SMT divergences, new week opening gaps, per-candle value area boxes, a small volume profile, delta candle colors and a checklist table.

How it works

Gaps are detected in series or one by one, with an ATR filter (0.1 by default). Filters can require a liquidity sweep, delivery from a higher-timeframe PD array, a macro time window, or that break-even was not breached. Gaps of 7 to 15 candles can be allowed.

Swing size for sweeps can follow the chart timeframe or be set by you. SMT uses a correlated symbol, automatic or manual, across several timeframes.

The volume tools estimate buy and sell imbalance per candle from lower-timeframe data.

How to use it

  • Use the grade and checklist to see which filters a setup meets.
  • Some traders watch IFVGs that form right after a sweep of a session or daily level.
  • Use equal highs and lows as reference pools where stops may cluster.
  • Turn modules on one at a time to learn what each adds.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

The script sends its own alert messages, and members can create an alert on them from the chart's alert dialog; alerts from indicators run while ZoroCharts is open in the browser.

Notes and limits

  • Results shown on the chart follow the script's own rules; Hide Losing Setups changes what you see, not what happened.
  • SMT and HTF tools need extra data requests and can slow loading.
  • Session times follow New York time.
