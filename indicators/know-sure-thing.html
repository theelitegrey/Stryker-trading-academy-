Know Sure Thing

TZTradeZoro OscillatorsFree Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

Know Sure Thing: a weighted blend of four smoothed rates of change, with a 9-bar signal line.

Overview

The Know Sure Thing (KST), by Martin Pring, combines momentum from four time spans into one line in its own pane, with a signal line in orange.

Colors show direction: green while KST is above its signal, red while below.

How it works

Take four rates of change (10, 15, 20 and 30 bars) and smooth them with simple averages (10, 10, 10 and 15). KST = RCMA1 x 1 + RCMA2 x 2 + RCMA3 x 3 + RCMA4 x 4.

The signal line is a 9-bar SMA of KST.

How to use it

  • Some traders watch KST crossing its signal line as a change in momentum.
  • Use zero-line crosses as broader momentum shifts.
  • Look for divergence between KST and price.
  • Use it on higher timeframes for a long-term momentum view.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

You can alert on its line crossing a value or the candle close (from the chart's + menu); checked while ZoroCharts is open.

Notes and limits

  • Works on any symbol and timeframe; it only needs candle data.
  • It is smooth and slow; turns appear after price has moved.
