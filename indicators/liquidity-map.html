Liquidity Map (order book heatmap)

TZTradeZoro Order flowPro v1.0 · Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

A full-screen map of resting order-book liquidity over time, with trade bubbles, a price ladder on the right and volume bars below.

In plain English

What it shows

  • A map of resting order-book size over time: the glow runs from deep blue (small) to yellow, orange and red (the biggest). A price line, trade bubbles, a book ladder and volume bars sit on top.

How traders use it

  • See where large orders waited and where they were removed.
  • Use the contrast and size filters to find the view you like.

What it does not tell you

  • It describes resting orders and prints. It does not predict anything.
  • Futures depth for this tool is limited for now; crypto works for Pro members.

Overview

Liquidity Map turns the chart into a view of the order book. The size resting at each price shows as a glow, from deep blue for small size to yellow, orange and red for the biggest walls.

A thin price line runs through it, with bubbles for traded volume, an order-book ladder on the right and volume bars along the bottom.

How it works

For crypto it reads the exchange's public order book and trade stream, plus recorded book history. Each time bucket becomes one column of the heatmap. A wall that is pulled or filled simply stops.

Trade bubbles are sized by traded volume and split green and red by the share of aggressive buying and selling. The right-hand columns show the current size per price and the running total from the best price outward.

How to use it

  • Use it to see where size is resting and whether price trades into it or away from it.
  • Some traders watch bubbles that appear right at a bright wall.
  • Use the ladder to read the size near the current price at a glance.
  • Turn Hide candles off if you want to keep your candles under the map.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Notes and limits

  • It describes resting orders and prints. It does not predict anything and has no order buttons.
  • Futures depth for this tool is limited to admins for now; crypto works for Pro members.
  • Contrast and size filters change only how the map looks, not the data.
