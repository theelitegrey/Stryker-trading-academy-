Moving Average Envelope

TZTradeZoro Moving averagesFree v1.0 · Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

A moving average with upper and lower bands set a fixed percent away, 2.5% around a 20-bar SMA by default.

Overview

The Moving Average Envelope draws a basis moving average with two bands, one above and one below, at a fixed percentage distance. By default the basis is a 20-bar SMA of the close and the bands sit 2.5% away.

Colors follow direction: bullish and bearish colors, with a gray basis.

How it works

Upper band = basis x (1 + percent / 100). Lower band = basis x (1 - percent / 100). The basis can be SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA (SMMA), HMA or VWMA.

Unlike Bollinger Bands, the width does not change with volatility; it is always the same share of the basis.

How to use it

  • Some traders watch touches of the bands as signs price is stretched from its average.
  • Use a close outside the band to spot strong moves.
  • Use the basis as the trend line and the bands as a range around it.
  • Adjust the percent to fit how much the symbol usually moves.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

You can alert on its line crossing a value or the candle close (from the chart's + menu); checked while ZoroCharts is open.

Notes and limits

  • Works on any symbol and timeframe; it only needs candle data.
  • A fixed percent may be too wide on quiet symbols and too narrow on volatile ones.
