Positive Volume Index

TZTradeZoro VolumeFree Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

Positive Volume Index: a running line that moves only on bars with higher volume than the bar before, with a 255-bar EMA.

Overview

The Positive Volume Index (PVI) tracks price change only on days when volume rises. It plots in its own pane with a 255-bar signal EMA.

Colors show its position against the EMA, with a gradient fill.

How it works

Start at 1000. On a bar with higher volume than the previous one, PVI changes by the same percent as the source (close by default). On other bars it stays the same.

The signal is a 255-bar EMA, about one trading year on daily charts. The starting value has no meaning; read the slope and the position against the EMA.

How to use it

  • Some traders read PVI above its EMA as a sign of a broad uptrend.
  • Compare it with the Negative Volume Index.
  • Use it on daily charts as designed.
  • Watch the color change when PVI crosses its EMA.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

You can alert on its line crossing a value or the candle close (from the chart's + menu); checked while ZoroCharts is open.

Notes and limits

  • It needs volume data; on symbols without volume it shows nothing useful.
  • Made for daily charts; the 255-bar EMA needs a long history.
