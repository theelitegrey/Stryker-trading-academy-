What the app does

Futures charts

Stryker Charts shows candlestick charts for futures (NQ, ES, GC, CL and more). A member can switch the futures feed to their own Rithmic login.

Two plants, read only

The client logs in to the Ticker Plant (last trade + best bid/offer) and the History Plant (time-bar replay). It never logs in to the Order or PnL plants.

No orders, ever

No order entry, no order or fill data, no account balances, no P&L. Education only. Not financial advice.

How to test it

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Use the form above (email or Google). No payment screen appears.

  2. 2

    Open Charts

    You are taken to strykertrading.com/charts. Rithmic is switched on for your account automatically about a minute after you sign up; reload Charts if the button isn't there yet.

  3. 3

    Connect broker

    Top-right of the chart: Connect broker. Choose system Rithmic Test, enter your Rithmic Test user ID and password, Connect.

  4. 4

    Watch it stream

    Futures charts reload from Rithmic history and the live candle follows Rithmic trades. The attribution strip and (i) notices show under the chart while connected. Disconnect returns to the standard feed.

Rithmic Test is a test server: prices may differ from the live market. Production systems (Rithmic 01, Rithmic 04 Colo, Rithmic Paper Trading) show as "available after approval".
