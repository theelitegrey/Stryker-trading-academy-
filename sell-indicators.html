Questions

What does it cost to sell?

0% platform fee for Founding Creators during launch; Stripe's standard fees apply. They come out of your sales.

Is my code safe?

It is hidden and traceable, not unbreakable. Members run it on their chart without seeing the source, and each delivered copy carries a hidden mark that can be traced to the account that loaded it. No protection is perfect, so only publish what you are comfortable sharing under those terms.

Which plans and billing can I offer?

Free, paid or invite-only access, charged monthly, yearly or one-time. You choose who gets each indicator.

How do I get paid?

Members pay you directly through Stripe. Payouts go to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule. You sell as an independent creator, and you handle refunds and taxes.

How does Discord work?

Connect your Discord server once and pick a role for each plan. Members link their Discord and get the role; when their access ends, the role is removed.
