Questions

Before you start.

What do I get for free?

Chapters 1–10, SPX same-day (0DTE) GEX, the Global Monitor, economic calendar and pre-market brief, a trade journal for up to 20 trades a month entered by hand, 3 replay sessions a week, and read access to the community. No card required.

What does Pro add?

All 64 chapters including the Volume Profile and Prop Firm tracks, full GEX (every symbol and expiry, futures conversion, strike table), an unlimited journal with import, broker sync, analytics and AI coach, unlimited replay, the Smart Money Desk, charts, trading models, private indicators, and posting and direct messages in the community.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Billing in Settings. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again.

What happens to my data on Free?

Your journal and progress stay saved. Nothing is deleted. You just see the Free limits.

Are the indicators trade signals?

No. The private indicators are granted to your TradingView username and mark the concepts the curriculum teaches. They are not trade signals.

Is this financial advice?

No. Stryker Trading Academy is an educational service only and does not provide financial advice or manage client funds. Trading carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors.
