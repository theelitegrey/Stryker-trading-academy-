Plots and chart colors

Everything that draws one value per bar. Call these at the top level of your script.

barcolor(color, offset=0, editable=true, show_last=na, title="", display="all")

Colors the candles. Call at the top level of the script.

Returns: nothing

ParameterTypeNotes
colorcolor
offsetintDefault 0
editableboolDefault true
show_lastintDefault na
titlestringDefault ""
displaystringDefault "all"

bgcolor(color, offset=0, editable=true, show_last=na, title="", display="all")

Colors the chart background of each bar. Call at the top level of the script.

Returns: nothing

ParameterTypeNotes
colorcolor
offsetintDefault 0
editableboolDefault true
show_lastintDefault na
titlestringDefault ""
displaystringDefault "all"

fill(plot1, plot2, color=na, title="", editable=true, show_last=na, fillgaps=false, display="all")

Fills the space between two plots or two hlines. Call at the top level of the script.

Returns: nothing

ParameterTypeNotes

plot2any
colorcolorDefault na
titlestringDefault ""
editableboolDefault true
show_lastintDefault na
fillgapsboolDefault false
displaystringDefault "all"

hline(price, title="", color=#787b86, linestyle="dashed", linewidth=1, editable=true, display="all")

Draws a horizontal line at a fixed price. Call at the top level of the script.

Returns: hlineid

displaystringDefault "all"
